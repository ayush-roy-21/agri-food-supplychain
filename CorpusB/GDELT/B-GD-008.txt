FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Indian organic food manufacturers exporting to the United States are facing mounting compliance costs under the FDA's Foreign Supplier Verification Programs (FSVP). US importers are requiring Indian MSME suppliers to undergo rigorous third-party food safety audits, hazard analyses, and microbial risk assessments. For smallholder organic cooperatives certified under India's National Programme for Organic Production (NPOP), hiring US-recognized preventive controls qualified individuals (PCQI) and maintaining bilingual HACCP documentation costs upwards of $15,000 annually. Exporters argue that these technical barriers to trade (TBT) disproportionately disadvantage small agribusinesses compared to multinational food conglomerates.
