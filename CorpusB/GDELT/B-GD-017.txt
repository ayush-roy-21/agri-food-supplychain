FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Fresh table grape exporters in Nashik and Sangli, Maharashtra, report significant compliance hurdles as European Union plant health authorities enforce strict phytosanitary inspection protocols for Lobesia botrana (grapevine moth) and pesticide residues. Under APEDA guidelines, exports to the EU are permitted exclusively from registered orchards processed through APEDA-recognized packhouses. However, small farmer producer companies (FPCs) allege that obtaining annual packhouse renewal and HortiNet inspection clearances takes over six weeks, forcing them to sell export-quality grapes in domestic markets at distressed prices.
