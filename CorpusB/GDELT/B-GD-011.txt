FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Small and medium cardamom exporters in Idukki and Cochin, Kerala, have raised concerns over the inclusion of Indian spices under Annex II of European Union Regulation (EU) 2019/1793, which mandates 20 percent physical border sampling at EU ports of entry. Exporters point out that even consignments accompanied by official Spices Board analytical certificates are subjected to destructive re-sampling at European border control posts. The resulting laboratory holds add three weeks to distribution timelines, causing volatile price drops for premium green cardamom grades in European wholesale markets.
