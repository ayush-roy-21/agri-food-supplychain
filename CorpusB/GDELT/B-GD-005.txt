FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Indian Basmati rice exporters are witnessing a sharp drop in European Union shipments following the European Commission's refusal to raise the Maximum Residue Limit (MRL) for tricyclazole, a fungicide commonly used by paddy farmers to control blast disease. The EU enforces an import tolerance of 0.01 mg/kg (default limit of analytical quantification), effectively acting as a zero-tolerance barrier. Exporters from Punjab and Haryana state that despite advisory campaigns by APEDA to shift farmers toward alternative molecules like azoxystrobin and isoprothiolane, cross-contamination during milling and contract farming enforcement remain major hurdles. Consignments failing pre-shipment laboratory assays are being diverted to Middle Eastern markets at discounted rates.
