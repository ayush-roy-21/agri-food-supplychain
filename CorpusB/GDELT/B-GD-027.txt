FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Export of Kesar mangoes from Gujarat to premium high-value markets such as Japan, South Korea, and Australia is being severely hampered by an acute shortage of APEDA-approved Vapor Heat Treatment (VHT) and Hot Water Treatment (HWT) facilities. Bilateral phytosanitary protocols mandate that all fruit must undergo supervised thermal treatment to eliminate fruit fly larvae before export. With only two operational VHT facilities in the region, MSME mango exporters face waiting lists of up to five days during peak harvest season, causing over-ripening and forcing exporters to divert produce to lower-value domestic markets.
