FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Indian aquaculture shrimp consignments exported to Japan are facing increased border rejections following the Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) enforcing a zero-tolerance default MRL of 0.001 ppm for furazolidone (AOZ) and ethoxyquin residues. A Tuticorin-based seafood processing MSME reported that two refrigerated containers were rejected at Yokohama port after trace levels of AOZ were detected, traced back to unauthorized veterinary premixes used by contract farmers in commercial shrimp feed. Exporter associations have urged MPEDA to institute mandatory batch testing and certification for all commercial aquaculture feed mills.
