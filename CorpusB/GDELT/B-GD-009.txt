FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
During the peak financial year-end export rush, the Spices Board Quality Evaluation Laboratory (QEL) in Navi Mumbai experienced severe sample congestion, extending analytical test reporting times from 3 days to over 9 working days. Multiple Maharashtra-based spice processors reported that delayed analytical reports prevented them from filing shipping bills on ICEGATE before vessel cut-off times at JNPT port. Exporter associations have petitioned the Ministry of Commerce to authorize additional private NABL-accredited laboratories to issue statutory export health certificates during peak seasonal demand.
