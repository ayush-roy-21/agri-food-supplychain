FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Seafood shipments originating from Indian processing plants in Kerala and Tamil Nadu are experiencing prolonged border holds at major European ports, including Vigo and Genoa, due to stringent scrutiny of Illegal, Unreported, and Unregulated (IUU) fishing catch certificates. European port authorities are cross-verifying vessel logbooks, landing declarations, and MPEDA fishing vessel registration numbers against satellite vessel monitoring system (VMS) data. Minor clerical discrepancies in vessel ownership names or gear specifications on the Export Inspection Council (EIC) health certificate result in immediate consignment detention, exposing Indian marine exporters to severe contractual penalties and container demurrage costs.
