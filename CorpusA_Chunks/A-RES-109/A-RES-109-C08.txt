2.2 Locating studies and evaluation
The articles for the study are identified and screened after formulating the research questions.
This section summarizes the steps followed for locating the study and its evaluation.
2.2.1 Time horizon . Articles published during 2002 – 2018 were considered for the study.
The works prior to 2002 were excluded due to the limited number of publications on the
supply chain issues in SME food sectors (Mahajan et al., 2017; Siddh et al., 2015). Being the
immediate previous year at the time of the review, we included all articles till the end of
the year 2018.
2.2.2 Selection of database . WoS database from Clarivate Analytics is used to collect the
articles. We selected the WoS database for the following solid reasons. The first reason is to
get a comprehensive result and to ensure the quality of articles. The second WoS database
includes more than 18,000 journals of high impact from six different citation databases.
Third, the retrieved result from WoS can be organized and analysed easily through
bibliometric software tools (Colicchia and Strozzi, 2012).
2.2.3 Search criteria for article selection . As proposed by Fahimnia et al. (2015), a three-
level keyword was used to extract articles as shown in Table II. “OR” connectors were used
to connect keywords in each level, and different levels of keywords were connected by using
“AND” connectors. To find root word plus, we used “*” symbol at the end of some words.
Different terms with their variants were used at each level of the search strings so as to not
limit the database search to a specific aspect of the research domain, as our primary
objective was to identify the major research concepts. For example, Level 1 keywords used
the term “supply chain ” including its variants such as “inventory”, “logistics”, “supply
network” or “value chain ”.
2.2.4 Sorting of articles . In order to include the most relevant articles, a set of inclusion
and exclusion criteria is defined, followed by Newbert (2007) as listed below:
(1) peer-reviewed scientific journals published only in English are included;
(2) articles published during 2002 – 2018 were considered for the study since articles that
met our selection criteria before 2002 were very limited;
(3) selected articles that contain at least one keyword in each level of the search string in
the title, abstract and keywords;
(4) commercial publications, book chapters, proceedings papers, periodicals and
working papers are not included; and
(5) articles that are not relevant to the SME FSC are excluded.
During the initial search, 3,733 articles are retrieved from the WoS database, and after
applying the inclusion and exclusion criteria (1 – 4), a total of 3,040 articles are retained. Both
the authors independently screened the papers on the basis of the title, abstract and
keywords. The papers for which both the authors agreed have been included. In case of any
discrepancy, we included a paper for full-text reading. Specifically, we excluded 1,949
Level 1 “supply chain*” or “inventory” or “logistic*” or “supply network” or “value chain”
Level 2 “small business*” or “sme” or “SMEs” or “small and medium ” or “msme” or “small
firm*” or “small industr*” or “small scale industr*” or “micro business” or “household
business” or “small organisation*” or “small compan*” or “small entit*” or “medium
compan*” or “small enterpri*” or “medium enterpri*” or “family business” or “small
retail*” or “small to medium ” or “farm*”
Level 3 “*food” or “beverage*” or “dairy” or “fruit*” or “vegetable*” or “meat” or “beef” or
“fish” or “halal” or “drinks”
