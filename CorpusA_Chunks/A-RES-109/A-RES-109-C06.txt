In the last few years, many food quality and safety issues are frequently reported. For
instance, the milk scam disclosed in India fou nd that saturated fatty oil and powder were
blended with the milk to increase the sale (Dandageet al., 2017). The FSC has received increasing
attention from both academia and practitioners,as consumers are concerned about food quality
a n ds a f e t y( M a h a j a net al., 2017). For a better understanding of this research domain, it is
important to map and summarize the existing knowledge base through an effective literature
review. However, reviews on this research area were focussed only on a specific food sector
(Ganeshkumaret al.,2 0 1 7 ;B e get al., 2017; Denham et al., 2015) or a particular issue like food
safety (Auler et al., 2017; Manning and Soon, 2016), food traceability (Dandage et al., 2017),
environmental issues (Borodin et al. ,2 0 1 6 ;D e n h a met al. , 2015; Wiedemann et al. , 2015a),
perishable food products (Siddhet al., 2015) and processed FSC (Prakash, 2018; Mahajanet al.,
2017). It is observed that none of the previous literature map and summarize the existing
knowledge base in the SME FSC domain (Luoet al., 2018). Most of the past reviews on the FSC
are narrative as shown in Table I. A systemati c literature review (SLR) is preferred, as it
outperforms other review methods since its characteristics, explanatory, heuristic nature,
transparency and inclusivity, eliminate an y errors and bias occurred, thereby making the
research result more objective (Denyer and Tranfield, 2009). To address this research gap, we
present a SLR of SME FSC. A total of 1,091 resea rch articles published from 2002 to 2018 are
collected from the Web of Science (WoS) database. We use bibliographic coupling (BC) combined
with co-word analysis to identify the historical relations of the research themes that emerged
during the periods 2002– 2014 and 2002– 2018. The broader thematic context is obtained for the
period 2002– 2014, whereas the inclusion of recent articles (i.e. 2015– 2018) resulted in identifying
the emerging research directions out of these broader issues.
