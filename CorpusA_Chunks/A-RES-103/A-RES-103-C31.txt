5.1.5.2 Milk 
47. The EIC and their EIA offices are responsible for the follow -up of non- compliances 
found in the milk residue monitoring plan. Furthermore, documented procedures for the 
management of non- compliant results are described in a specific EIC executive 
instruction for milk (31), that includes key elements as applicable in the EU (32). 
48. A significant proportion of the residue monitoring samples analysed in the last three 
years for Group B3d ( aflatoxin M1) were  non-compliant ((2 018/2019: 90 samples and 
34 non- compliances (3 7%), 2019/2020: 90 samples and 10 non- compliances (11%), 
2020/2021: 51 samples and 19 non- compliances (37%)) . There were no  residue 
monitoring plan non-compliances for any other substance group in the last three years, 
though that might b e due to the fact that instructions were in place that clearly limited 
the effectiveness of detecting substances included in Group A6 (banned antimicrobials), 
Group B1 (antimicrobials) and Group B2a (anthelmintics) (see finding 14 and 67).
