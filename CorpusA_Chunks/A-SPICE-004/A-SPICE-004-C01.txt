Sub : Submission of pending Quarterly Export Returns (QER) for 2023
24, 2024-25 & 2025-26 — reg.
This has reference to the subject cited above.
As per Sub Regulation S(9) of the Spices Board (Registration of Exporters)
Regulation, 1989, the certificate holder shall submit quarterly returns
regarding export and import of spices in the prescribed form specified by the
Board from time to time and non-submission of Quarterly Export Returns shall
be treated as a violation of the said regulation.
In this regard it has been observed that Quarterly Export Returns (QERs) have
not been filed by many of the registered exporters of Spices & Spice products
during the period 2023-24, 2024-25 and 2025-26 (upto 34 quarter). The matter
has already been brought to the notice of all the exporters through various
modes including notifications in the Export Support System (ESS), email
communications and WhatsApp messages.
In view of the above, all the registered exporters of spices/spice products are
hereby requested to ensure that the pending export returns for the period
2023-24,2024-25 2025-26 are filed online without fail,
In this regard, it is reiterated that the Board has already implemented an online
system for submission of QER during 2021 vide Circular No.4/2021-22 dated
17.06.2021, wherein exporters can upload their Excel file in the Export Support
System (ESS) of the Board under the tab “Quarterly Returns.” The prescribed
template for submission is available in the portal. The link for submission of
QERs is given below.
https: / /indianspices.org.in/ESS/
It is also mandated that in case there is no export/purchase/import during a
quarter, the exporter shall submit a NIL return to that effect to be filed in the
portal.
Sasa / Email : mail.sboard@gov.in aaqaee / Website : www.indianspices.com

[Page 2]
Since the QER module forms part of the Export Support System (ESS) of the
Board, non-submission of Quarterly Returns will restrict access to the ESS
portal. In order to avoid such restriction, exporters who have pending
submissions are requested to file the QERs on or before 15th April 2026.
This circular is issued to bring to the notice of exporters whose Quarterly
Export Returns are pending for submission, in accordance with the above
regulation and to ensure compliance.
Effect: Exporters are hereby informed that non-submission of Quarterly Export
Returns within the stipulated time will be treated as violation of the Spices -
Board (Registration of Exporters) Regulation, 1989 and may invite regulatory
action including restriction of ESS services. The access of ESS will be restricted
with effect from 16t April 2026.
