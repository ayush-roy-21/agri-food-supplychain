Policy support
West Bengal is host to prominent public and 
private enterprises due to its proximity to rich 
iron ore reserves in neighbouring states and 
coal deposits within West Bengal. Facilities like 
the Plasto Steel Park in Barjora, Bankura offers 
scope of further expansion and investment in this 
sector in West Bengal. Given the substantial land 
requirements of this industry, the government 
has devised the Land Bank Policy to facilitate the 
allocation of land for industrial purposes.
