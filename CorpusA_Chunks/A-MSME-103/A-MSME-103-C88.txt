 Diversification of the export basket of a state helps it increase stability, as 
it becomes resilient to market shocks, and increase channels of revenue.

4.2.1519.9524.0226.81  Export Concentration
Product Concentration Index is used to measure the dispersion of trade

vulnerability to trade shocks
4.2.2551.0440.3333.04  Market Penetration Index
Market Penetration Index measures the extent to which exports from 
a State reach already proven markets. It is calculated as the number of 
countries to which a State exports a particular product divided by the 
number of total countries that import that product in a year.
