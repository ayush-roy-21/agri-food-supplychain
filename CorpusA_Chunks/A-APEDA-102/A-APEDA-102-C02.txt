Subject: Extension in suspension of Importation of Rice by the Philippines -reg.
Philippines had imposed a 60-day suspension on importation of well-milled and regular-milled

rice varieties such as Basmati or fragrant rice were excluded from the purview of import- |

above mentioned rice imports till the end of this year (31 Dec 2025).
a In view of the above, APEDA shall for the time being suspend issuance of RCAC for
Export of milled non-Basmati Rice to the Philippines, till the said import restriction is revoked by
the Philippines Government.
