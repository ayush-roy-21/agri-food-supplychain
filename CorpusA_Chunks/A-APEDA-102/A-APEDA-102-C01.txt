ADVISORY
Subject: Advisory for rice exports to Philippines- reg.
This has reference to the APEDA Advisory CRL-2020-21-000031 dated 21.11.2025 regarding
the extension in suspension of importation of rice by the Philippines and thereby, the temporary
suspension of issuance of RCAC for Export of milled non-Basmati Rice to the Philippines (Copy
enclosed).
Subsequent to the issuance of the above Advisory, in a press release issued by the Department of
Agriculture, Government of the Philippines concerning import of rice, it is stated that:

a. Bureau of Plant Industry will begin processing applications for Sanitary and
Phytosanitary Import Clearances (SPICs) covering about 500,000 metric tons of rice,
including the 50,000 metric tons reserved for Government Agencies.

b. All shipments must arrive by mid-February to prevent imported rice from weighing on
palay prices at the start of the summer harvest.

c. When importation resumes, tariffs will rise to 20 percent from 15 percent.

d. Rice imports during the January - February window will be limited to 17 ports
nationwide: Manila, Batangas, Tacloban, Bacolod, Iligan, Cagayan de Oro, Davao,
Zamboanga, Cebu, Iloilo, Capiz, Tagbilaran, Dumaguete, Subic, Calbayog, General
Santos, and Tabaco.

In the light of the above, APEDA will resume the issuance of RCAC’s for export of milled non-
Basmati rice to the Philippines with immediate effect. Further, it is possible that the Government
of the Philippines may issue the relevant notifications concerning the above measures in due
course.
However, stakeholders are also requested to watch over the latest developments in
Philippines for any change in the policy conditions related to rice. In case of any doubt, it is
advised to consult Eol(Manila)/APEDA, as a note of abundant caution, before deciding on
the shipment.
