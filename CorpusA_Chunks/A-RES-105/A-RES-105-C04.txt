Institutional Theory 
“Organizations are affected by external social forces 
(coercive, mimetic and normative) to implement 
social and environmental responsibility and alter their 
activities to achieve credibility. Companies maintain 
the consistency of their business activities with the 
aspirations of society by adapting to legislation and 
imitating their rivals” 
(Joshi et al., 2020; Khoja 
et al., 2022; Klewitz et al., 
2014;Sharma et al., 2019; 
Shibin et al., 2020)
