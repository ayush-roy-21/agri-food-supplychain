A senior official on Tuesday said the development aligns with broader efforts to increase the global visibility of agricultural products from the region and integrate farmers into international value chains. The Northeast's diverse horticultural produce has increasingly attracted attention due to its quality and unique geographical characteristics. Union Commerce and Industry Minister Piyush Goyal welcomed the development earlier on Monday, highlighting the significance of the export shipment. In a post on X, Goyal said, "Assam's renowned GI-tagged Tezpur litchi is now spreading its sweetness in foreign markets. With the support of APEDA, the first export shipment of Tezpur litchi has departed for Dubai." He added that the initiative would help provide global recognition to the region's litchi, ensure better prices for farmers and create fresh opportunities for agricultural products from Northeast India in international markets. ================================================================================
