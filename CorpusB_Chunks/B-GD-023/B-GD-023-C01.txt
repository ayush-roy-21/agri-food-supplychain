The International Whisky Competition has just announced its 2026 results, judging entries blind on a 100-point scale and awarding 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place in each category. Indian whisky has its own set of categories here, and today we are going to look at the two producers that swept the awards. Two producers account for all six of the top placements. The two groups represent different approaches to Indian whisky, demonstrating that, even as Indian single malts dominate headlines and competitions, blends still have something to offer.
