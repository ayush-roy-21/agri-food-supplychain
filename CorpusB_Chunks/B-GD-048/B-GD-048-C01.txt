Vietnam’s 5% broken rice RI-VNBKN5-P1 was offered at $405-$415 per metric ton on Thursday, compared with $415-$420 a week ago. “Demand is weak, with the largest buyer, Philippines, suspending rice imports from Vietnam in June,” a trader based in Ho Chi Minh City said. “We heard they will resume imports of Vietnamese rice in July, but with volume capped at around 200,000 tons.” Meanwhile, India’s 5% broken parboiled variety RI-INBKN5-P1 was quoted at $337-$342 per ton this week, up from 335-$340 last week.
