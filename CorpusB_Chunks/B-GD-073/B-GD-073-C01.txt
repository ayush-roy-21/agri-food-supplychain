The Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (APEDA) is banking on upcoming free trade agreements (FTAs), agri-start-ups and farmer producer organisations (FPOs) to drive the next phase of growth in India's agricultural exports, APEDA Chairman Abhishek Dev said.
