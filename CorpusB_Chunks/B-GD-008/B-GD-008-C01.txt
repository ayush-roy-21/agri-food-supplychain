India has been included in the European Union’s revised draft list for continued export of aquaculture products to the 27-nation bloc, a move that is expected to ensure uninterrupted shipments of these goods beyond September this year, an official statement said on Thursday. The revised draft list was published by the European Union on May 12 after India undertook compliance measures in line with the European Commission Delegated Regulation, which requires exporting countries to ensure that animals and animal products exported to the EU are free from the use of antimicrobial medicines. Earlier, India was omitted from the EU’s implementing regulation issued on October 4, 2024, which had not included the country among third nations authorised to export products of animal origin intended for human consumption to the EU from September 2026.
