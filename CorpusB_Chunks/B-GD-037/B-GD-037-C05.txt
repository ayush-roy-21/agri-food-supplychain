Eating the Past: The botany of vanilla: Nutmeg was eventually cultivated on the Réunion Island off Madagascar, the same island discussed in a previous Eating the Past episode on the cultivation of vanilla. As the story goes, horiculturalist and missionary Pierre Poivre found his way to the Banda Islands in the 1750s, despite confusing Dutch maps intentionally meant to deceive navigators about the location of these spice islands. He broke the Dutch monopoly on nutmeg trade by smuggling out more than 3000 nutmeg plants and reestablishing them in the French-controlled islands of Marutitius and Réunion off the coast of east Africa. ================================================================================
