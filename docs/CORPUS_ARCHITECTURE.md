# The Barrier Horizon: Sustainable Supplier Hurdles Facing Indian Agri-Food MSMEs
## Corpus Architecture & Institutional Pillar Breakdown

This document details the structural design, routing logic, and thematic scope of **The Barrier Horizon: Sustainable Supplier Hurdles Facing Indian Agri-Food MSMEs (CorpusA)**.

---

## 1. Core Research Questions (RQs) Addressed

CorpusA is organized to provide empirical data across four foundational research dimensions:

- **RQ1 (Domestic Institutional Governance & Convergence)**: How do statutory mandates and inspection schemes intersect across primary commodity boards (APEDA, Spices Board, MPEDA), certification authorities (EIC), and domestic licensing bodies (FSSAI)?
- **RQ2 (Bilateral Export Friction & SPS/TBT Non-Tariff Barriers)**: How do destination import regulations (EU DG SANTE, US FDA/NOAA, Japan MHLW) impact consignment clearances, sampling frequencies, and border rejection rates?
- **RQ3 (Digital Traceability & Compliance Automation)**: How are national digital infrastructures (TraceNet, HortiNet, e-SANTA, FoSCoS, e-CoO) evolving to meet real-time international supply chain verification?
- **RQ4 (Emerging Sustainability & Due Diligence Regimes)**: How are Indian agri-food exporters adapting to mandatory environmental and social due diligence frameworks (EUDR geolocation traceability, CSDDD, MSME ZED certification, and corporate social responsibility spending)?

## 2. Institutional Pillar Breakdown

### Pillar 1: APEDA (`A-APEDA-`)
- **Institution**: Agricultural and Processed Food Products Export Development Authority (Ministry of Commerce & Industry).
- **Mandate**: Export promotion and quality control for horticulture, organic products, and value-added processed foods.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-APEDA-001`: NPOP National Programme for Organic Production Certification Framework.
  - `A-APEDA-002`: TraceNet Farm Registration & Digital Traceability SOP.
  - `A-APEDA-004`: Recognition Scheme for Packhouses & Cold Storages.
  - `A-APEDA-005`: HortiNet Operational Guidelines for Mango, Grapes, and Vegetables.

### Pillar 2: Spices Board (`A-SPICE-`)
- **Institution**: Spices Board of India (Ministry of Commerce & Industry).
- **Mandate**: Regulatory inspection and mandatory sampling for export consignments of Indian spices.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-SPICE-001` to `A-SPICE-005`: Core quality evaluation, analytical charges, and export inspection guidelines.
  - `A-SPICE-006`: Mandatory Salmonella Testing for Chilli & Cumin Exported to USA.
  - `A-SPICE-007`: UK Official Certificate Requirements w.e.f. Jan 2026.
  - `A-SPICE-008`: EU Cumin Monitoring Mechanism for Pesticide Residues.

### Pillar 3: MPEDA (`A-MPEDA-`)
- **Institution**: Marine Products Export Development Authority (Ministry of Commerce & Industry).
- **Mandate**: Promotion, vessel registration, and seafood safety verification for marine products exports.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-MPEDA-001` to `A-MPEDA-005`: Statutory registration, vigilance, and export impact evaluation reports.
  - `A-MPEDA-006`: EU Catch Certificate SOP & IUU Fishing Validation Guidelines.
  - `A-MPEDA-007`: US NOAA Form DS-2031 Exporter Declaration & Turtle Excluder Device (TED) Inspection Protocol.
  - `A-MPEDA-008`: Pre-Harvest Test (PHT) Manual for Online Antibiotic Residue Monitoring in Aquaculture Shrimp.

### Pillar 4: EIC (`A-EIC-`)
- **Institution**: Export Inspection Council of India (Ministry of Commerce & Industry).
- **Mandate**: Official export certification body operating under Section 3 of the Export (Quality Control and Inspection) Act, 1963.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-EIC-001`: Export Act 1963 Governance Framework & Statutory Powers of EIAs.
  - `A-EIC-002`: Executive Instructions for Marine Products Health Certification to EU & USA.
  - `A-EIC-003`: Consignment-Wise Inspection & Health Certification Scheme for Spices to EU.
  - `A-EIC-004`: Laboratory Recognition Scheme & Analytical Testing Protocol Manual (ISO/IEC 17025).
  - `A-EIC-005`: Approved Technologist Scheme & In-Process Quality Control (IPQC).

### Pillar 5: FSSAI (`A-FSSAI-`)
- **Institution**: Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (Ministry of Health & Family Welfare).
- **Mandate**: Apex domestic food safety regulatory body governing processing standards and third-party auditing.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-FSSAI-001`: Licensing & Registration Regulations Schedule 4 Hygiene & HACCP Mandate.
  - `A-FSSAI-002`: Food Safety Auditing Regulations, 2018 (Recognition of Third-Party HACCP/FSSC 22000 Auditing).
  - `A-FSSAI-003`: FoSCoS Central Licensing Procedures for 100% Export Oriented Units (EOUs).
  - `A-FSSAI-004`: Inter-Agency Regulatory Cooperation Framework (FSSAI-APEDA-MPEDA-EIC).
  - `A-FSSAI-005`: Manual of Methods of Analysis & Laboratory Recognition Scheme.

### Pillar 6: DGFT & India Trade Portal (`A-DGFT-`)
- **Institution**: Directorate General of Foreign Trade & Federation of Indian Export Organisations (FIEO).
- **Mandate**: Foreign Trade Policy formulation, IEC licensing, and trade barrier mapping.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-DGFT-001`: Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) 2023 Chapter 2 General Provisions & IEC Mandate.
  - `A-DGFT-002`: Public Notice on Online Issuance of Certificate of Origin (e-CoO).
  - `A-DGFT-003`: India Trade Portal SPS & TBT Compliance Matrix for Spices and Marine Products.
  - `A-DGFT-004`: Mandatory Quality Control Orders (QCOs) & Pre-Shipment Export Clearance Notification.
  - `A-DGFT-005`: RoDTEP Scheme Operational Guidelines for Food Processors.

### Pillar 7: EU Tier / DG SANTE (`A-EU-`)
- **Institution**: European Commission Directorate-General for Health and Food Safety (EUR-Lex).
- **Mandate**: Enforcing EU import hygiene, emergency border controls, and rapid alert systems.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-EU-001`: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2019/1793 (Emergency controls on Indian spices).
  - `A-EU-002`: Council Regulation (EC) No 1005/2008 (IUU Fishing Catch Certificate regime).
  - `A-EU-003`: Regulation (EC) No 853/2004 (Specific hygiene rules for marine processing).
  - `A-EU-004`: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2021/405 (Approved third countries & residue monitoring).
  - `A-EU-005`: Rapid Alert System for Food and Feed (RASFF) SOP & Border Rejection Mechanism.

### Pillar 8: Data.gov.in Trade Statistics (`A-DATA-`)
- **Institution**: Open Government Data Platform India / DGCIS.
- **Mandate**: Structured statistical datasets tracking agricultural export volumes and values.
- **Key File**:
  - `A-DATA-001.csv`: Multi-year export statistics for HS Code 0306 (Marine Shrimp), 0904/0909 (Spices), and 2009 (Processed Foods) across top destination markets.

### Pillar 9: NITI Aayog & MSME ZED (`A-ZED-`)
- **Institution**: Ministry of MSME & National Institution for Transforming India (NITI Aayog).
- **Mandate**: Promoting sustainable manufacturing practices and cluster-based agri-export infrastructure.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-ZED-001`: MSME Sustainable (ZED) Certification Scheme Guidelines for Food Processing Enterprises.
  - `A-ZED-002`: NITI Aayog Strategy for Doubling Agri-Exports & MSME Infrastructure Integration.

### Pillar 10: National CSR Portal (`A-CSR-`)
- **Institution**: Ministry of Corporate Affairs (csr.gov.in).
- **Mandate**: Governance of corporate social responsibility expenditure under Companies Act, 2013.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-CSR-001`: Companies Act Section 135 Statutory Mandate & Form CSR-2 E-Filing Format.
  - `A-CSR-002`: MCA Guidance on Eligible CSR Spending for Rural Agricultural & Cold-Chain Infrastructure.

### Pillar 11: EUDR & CSDDD Deforestation Regime (`A-EUDR-`)
- **Institution**: European Commission Directorate-General for Environment & Ministry of Commerce (India).
- **Mandate**: Enforcing zero-deforestation supply chains and human rights due diligence.
- **Key Files**:
  - `A-EUDR-001`: Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 (EUDR) Mandatory Geolocation Traceability & Due Diligence Statements.
  - `A-EUDR-002`: Directive (EU) 2024/1760 (CSDDD) Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence.
  - `A-EUDR-003`: Ministry of Commerce & APEDA Advisory on EUDR Compliance & GPS Plot Geotagging.

## 3. Storage Architecture & Redundancy

To prevent accidental data loss during multi-stage processing, CorpusA implements a **triple-redundant directory structure**:
1. `CorpusA/<Pillar>/`: Primary working document directory for easy inspection.
2. `data/CorpusA/<Pillar>/`: Mirrored analytical directory containing institutional sub-registries (`*_scrape_registry.json`).
3. `data/raw/CorpusA/<Pillar>/`: Immutable raw file backup preserving original scraped artifacts.
