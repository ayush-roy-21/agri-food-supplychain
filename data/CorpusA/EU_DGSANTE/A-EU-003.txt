OFFICIAL JOURNAL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION
REGULATION (EC) No 853/2004 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL
laying down specific hygiene rules for food of animal origin

1. OBJECTIVE & HACCP COMPLIANCE LINKAGE:
Supplementing Regulation (EC) No 852/2004 on general hygiene of foodstuffs, Regulation 853/2004 establishes specialized, enforceable sanitary standards for food business operators handling fish and fishery products destined for the European market.

2. SECTION VIII HYGIENE REQUIREMENTS FOR FISHERY PRODUCTS:
- Vessel Infrastructure: Factory vessels and freezer vessels must maintain dedicated freezing equipment capable of rapidly lowering core temperatures to -18 degrees Celsius or colder.
- Landing Ports & Auction Halls: Must maintain enclosed temperature-controlled receiving zones separate from fuel depot contamination.
- Processing Plants & Freezer Establishments: Filleting, peeling, and heading operations must proceed swiftly under chilled conditions (+4 degrees Celsius).

3. PARASITE & HISTAMINE CONTROLS:
Operators processing marine species prone to histamine formation (Scombridae, Clupeidae) must implement systematic batch sampling verifying histamine levels remain below statutory limits (100 mg/kg average). Raw fish intended for undercooked consumption must undergo mandatory freezing treatments (-20 degrees Celsius for 24 hours) to destroy viable zoonotic parasites.
