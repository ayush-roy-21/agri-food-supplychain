NATIONAL INSTITUTION FOR TRANSFORMING INDIA (NITI AAYOG)
STRATEGIC ROADMAP FOR AGRI-FOOD EXPORTS & MSME CLUSTER DEVELOPMENT

1. STRUCTURAL BOTTLENECKS IN INDIAN AGRI-EXPORTS:
The NITI Aayog policy framework identifies high post-harvest losses and fragmented cold-chain infrastructure as primary constraints limiting Indian spices, marine products, and processed foods from achieving full export potential.

2. CLUSTER-BASED EXPORT INTEGRATION:
To achieve global competitiveness, NITI Aayog mandates the alignment of Ministry of Food Processing Industries (MoFPI) Mega Food Parks with APEDA/MPEDA export hubs. MSME processing clusters are integrated with accredited common testing laboratories (NABL/EIC) to lower compliance testing costs for small food exporters.

3. DIGITAL TRACEABILITY RECOMMENDATIONS:
The report recommends nationwide scaling of blockchain and IoT-based farm-to-shipment traceability architectures—specifically expanding APEDA TraceNet and MPEDA HortiNet/AquaNet models to cover 100% of commercial value-added food exports to the EU and USA.
