MINISTRY OF MICRO, SMALL & MEDIUM ENTERPRISES (MSME)
GUIDELINES ON MSME SUSTAINABLE (ZED - ZERO DEFECT ZERO EFFECT) CERTIFICATION SCHEME

1. SCHEME PURPOSE & ZERO DEFECT ZERO EFFECT MANDATE:
Notified by the Ministry of MSME, the ZED Certification Scheme encourages micro, small, and medium agricultural and food processing enterprises to upgrade quality standards ('Zero Defect') and environmental sustainability ('Zero Effect').

2. MATURITY LEVELS & FOOD SAFETY SUBSIDIES:
The scheme classifies food processing units into three certification tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold). Enterprises achieving ZED Gold status receive direct financial subsidies up to 80% (and 90% for women/SC/ST entrepreneurs) covering ISO 22000, HACCP, FSSC 22000, and BRCGS international process certification auditing fees.

3. EXPORT CAPABILITY & MARKET LINKAGE:
ZED certified agri-food processors receive priority grading under APEDA value-added registration schemes and preferential access to international buyer-seller meets organized by Indian missions abroad.
