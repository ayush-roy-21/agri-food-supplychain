TRADE ADVISORY.01/2026-27
Sub : Compliance with Taiwan's Pesticide  Residue Standards for Export of Spices and
Spice Products from India  -Trade Advisory -reg
This  is to  bring to the attention  of all  Exporters of Spices & Spice  Products that the Taiwan
Food    and    Drug   Administration    (TFDA)    has    repeatedly   reported    instances   of   non-
compliance  in  consignments  of  spices  and  spice  products  exported  from  India.  These
non-compliances   are    primarily   related   to    pesticide    residue    limits    prescribed    under
Taiwan's    regulations.    The    notification    received    from    the    Taiwan    Food    and    Drug
Administration,  Ministry of Health and Welfare is enclosed  as (Armex I)
In   accordance   with   Article   26   of   Taiwan's   Regulations   governing   the   inspection   of
imported  foods  and  related  products,  Spices  Board  has  been  requested  to  strengthen
control   measures.   The   Board   has   also   been   r('quested   to   furnish   a   detailed   report
indicating  the  reasons for such  non-compliance,  along  with  the  corrective  and  preventive
measures undertaken.
Further,  as  per  Article  27  of  the  said  Regulations,  continued  non-compliance  will  invite
stringent   regulatory   action,   including   suspension   of  acceptance   of   import   inspection
applications for the affected  products by Taiwan, thereby impacting market access.
In  view  of  the  above,  all  exporters  of  spices  and  spice  products  are  hereby  directed  to
ensure   strict   compliance   to   Taiwan's   pesticide   residue   standards.   The   Standards   for
Pesticide  residue  Limits  in  Foods  (Regulation  No.  1141302806  dt.  November  26,  2025)  is
enclosed  as Annex 11 for information.
Exporters shall exercise due diligence and ensure that all  consignments comply with the  .
prescribed  limits prior to export.
