MINISTRY OF CORPORATE AFFAIRS (MCA)
GENERAL CIRCULAR ON SCHEDULE VII ELIGIBLE ACTIVITIES: RURAL DEVELOPMENT & AGRI-FOOD SUPPLY CHAIN CAPACITY

1. SCHEDULE VII ALIGNMENT FOR AGRIBUSINESS:
Item (iv) and Item (x) of Schedule VII authorize CSR spending on projects aimed at eradicating extreme hunger and malnutrition, promoting rural agricultural development, and enhancing agro-ecological sustainability.

2. INVESTMENT IN COLD-CHAIN & POST-HARVEST INFRASTRUCTURE:
Corporate food exporters are permitted to deploy statutory CSR funds to build community-owned solar packhouses, pre-cooling collection centers, and organic quality assaying laboratories in rural agrarian clusters, provided such facilities remain public assets managed by smallholding Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs).

3. PROHIBITION OF DIRECT COMMERCIAL BENEFITS:
Projects undertaken in pursuance of normal business operations or activities that exclusively benefit company employees or proprietary supply chains do not qualify as eligible CSR expenditure under Rule 2(1)(d).
