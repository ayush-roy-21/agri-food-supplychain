The number of reviewed articles published in leading journals is shown in Table III.
Among the leading journals, British Food Journal , Supply Chain Management – An
International Journal and Food Policy have published more number of papers during the
period 2002– 2014. However, during the period 2015– 2018, the Journal of Cleaner Production,
British Food Journal , Sustainability, Agricultural Systems , Food Control and International
Food and Agribusiness Management Review have contributed significantly. It is observed
that the Journal of Rural Studies is among the top contributing journals with publications
related to alternative and local food network, even though it is of limited relevance to the
research domain.
The distribution of reviewed papers by countries is shown in Table IV. It is found that
most of the research articles are from developed countries where the USA accounts for
19.25 per cent of the publications, making it the country with the most publications globally,
followed by the UK (10.82 per cent), Italy (10.45 per cent), Germany (7.15 per cent), Australia
(6.87 per cent) and the Netherlands (6.14 per cent). Among the developing countries, China
(5.41 per cent) and India (4.86 per cent) lead on the publications. Developed countries have
the advanced infrastructure, availability of funds and research environment, whereas the
limited funds, infrastructure and awareness result in a very few research publications in
developing countries (Siddh et al. , 2015).
The frequently used keywords are shown in Table V. Since authors use different words
to describe the same meaning, keywords often should be standardized. By following Su and
Lee (2010), the keywords are standardized in such a way that plural words are converted to
singular form, and similar words such as “SCM”, “value chain”, “supply chain management”
are standardized to “supply chain management ” on the basis of the popularity.
