This paper is organized as follows. Section 2 presents the proposed review methodology and
the steps followed for locating the study. Result and discussion of BC are elaborated in
Section 3. Finally, Section 4 concludes the paper by highlighting current research focus and
future research directions.
2. Review methodology
A SLR methodology is proposed to identify the major supply chain issues in SME food
industry. A four-phase SLR methodology (see Figure 1) is applied following the work of
Denyer and Tranfield (2009).
2.1 Research design
The methods of BC, co-word and network analysis are used to answer the following
four research questions, which are appropriate and influence the goal and focus of
the review:
RQ1. What are the major supply chain issues/themes addressed in the SME
food industry?
RQ2. What are the current research trends in the SME FSC?
RQ3. What are the different modelling approaches used in SME FSC?
RQ4. Which food sector has received attention in the literature?
