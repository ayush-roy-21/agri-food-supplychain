Sridhar A, Ponnuchamy M, Kumar PS, Kapoor A, Nguyen Vo DV, 
Rangasamy G: Digitalization of the agro-food sector for 
achieving sustainable development goals: a review. Sustain 
Food Technol 2023, 1:783-802. 
Publication highlights the significance of food security and agricultural 
sustainability in the face of rising food demand, inequality, climate 
change, and pandemics. The authors suggest that DTs can solve 
challenges in the agro-food sector, enhance production and supply 
chain, and achieve sustainable development goals. They propose in -
tegrating AI, precision farming, and big data analytics to boost yield and 
sustainability. Digitalization in agriculture can also cut costs and waste, 
while improvements in food safety and electronic commerce are an -
ticipated, with potential advancements in blockchain technology for 
improved traceability and personalized nutrition.

Balyan S, Jangir H, Tripathi SN, Tripathi A, Jhang T, Pandey P: 
Seeding a sustainable future: navigating the digital horizon of 
smart agriculture. Sustainability 2024, 16:475. 
Publication highlights the importance of agriculture for human survival 
but acknowledges that it is facing challenges due to climate change. The 
agriculture industry is using various measures and technologies to 
adapt, including plant breeding, robotics, and big data. The digitalization 
wave offers opportunities for smart farming but also presents challenges 
such as high implementation costs and data security concerns. The shift 
to digital farming offers solutions for environmental issues and economic 
growth, with future advancements potentially including data encryption 
and economic policies.

Publication discusses the challenges faced by the agriculture food 
supply chain (AFSC), including food safety, sustainability, government

the agro-food supply chain network and investigates performance

technology adoption and integration, as well as the digital transforma -
tion of AFSC. It emphasizes other research trends and identifies pos -
sible future challenges.
