Circular agri-food system
Agri-food systems encompass a wide range of activities, 
starting from the primary production of food and non- 
food agricultural products to their storage, post-harvest 
handling, transportation, processing, distribution, mar -
keting, consumption, and disposal/reuse — see Figure 2 . 
Creating a circular agri-food system requires diverting 
end-of-life products from being discarded as waste and 
instead replacing primary input resources with secondary 
materials obtained through the reprocessing of these 
end-of-life products in closed-loop supply chains [9] . 
The creation of a circular system is closely linked to the 
concept of a circular economy, where a circular economy 
is a regenerative system that minimizes resource waste, 
gas emissions, and energy leakage by slowing down, 
closing, and narrowing material and energy loops [10] .
The circular economy can have a significant impact on 
the agri-food system. By adopting the principles of the 
circular economy, organizations can design an organiza -
tional architecture that not only considers economic 
value but also sustains the environmental and social 
value embedded in the product or service throughout its 
entire lifecycle [11] . By promoting the principles of re -
duction, reuse, and recycling, the circular economy aims 
to minimize waste and maximize resource efficiency in
