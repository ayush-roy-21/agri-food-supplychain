MODULE 1 - INTRODUCTION TO FOOD PROCESSING SECTOR
COMPILED AND PREPARED FOR PMFME BENEFICIARY TRAINING
 
 
Key Benefits: 
 Enabling traditional artisans to deliver sustainable employment. 
 Financial Support: Government of India support up to INR 5 cr. for more than 500 
artisans and INR 2.5 cr. up to 500 artisans. 
 Enabling in setting up production facilities with modern machineries. Providing raw 
material support. Other support including marketing -business development and 
digitization. 
 Soft Interventions up to INR 25 lakhs for skill development, exposure visits, buyer 
seller meets. 
Scheme Applicable for: 
 Present artisans from traditional industries 
 Cluster of artisans in sectors such as Handicraft, Textile, Agro -Processing, Bamboo, 
Honey, Coir, Khadi etc. 
Detailed guidelines is available on https://sfurti.msme.gov.in/SFURTI/Home.aspx
