 Over the years, agricultural production in India has consistently recorded higher

 During the last 5 years ending 2018 -19, FPI has been growing at an Average Annual

 The share of FPI in total FDI has increased to 16.39 per cent in the recent years. 
 FDI - an important catalyst for economic growth in developing country  
 Foreign investment inflows are supplementing the scarcity of domestic investment in 
food processing sector.

Exports of processed food in FPI Sector
