Conclusion
The India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement reflects a defining moment in India’s trade
diplomacy, opening new avenues for comprehensive economic cooperation. By securing improved market
access for Indian goods, expanding opportunities in services and mobility, and deepening collaboration in
agriculture, investment and emerging sectors, the Agreement delivers tangible and wide-ranging benefits
across the economy.
From farmers and MSMEs to students and skilled professionals, the gains from this Agreement are
expected to be broad-based, reinforcing India’s position as a trusted, forward-looking global partner and
advancing the vision of a globally integrated Viksit Bharat 2047.
