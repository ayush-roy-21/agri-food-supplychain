7 CLOSING MEETING 
A closing meeting was held on 29 October 2022 with representatives of the central 
competent authority. At this meeting, the audi t team presented the main findings and 
preliminary conclusions of the audit. The authorities offered some clarifications and 
stated that they would take actions to address the shortcomings identified during the 
audit. 
8 RECOMMENDATIONS 
The competent authority is invited to provide details of the actions taken and planned, 
including deadlines for their completion ('action plan'), aimed at addressing the 
recommendations set out below, within 25 working days of receipt of this audit report.   
 
No Recommendation
