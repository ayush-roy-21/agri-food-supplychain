Policy support
West Bengal boasts a rich heritage of crafting 
handmade gold jewellery, with a substantial 
presence of highly skilled artisans and goldsmiths. 
At Ankurhati, Domjur in the Howrah district, the 
West Bengal Industrial Development Corporation 
Limited (WBIDC) has constructed a Gems & 
Jewellery Park on a 5.76-acre property. The 
area boasts a sizable concentration of trained 
and semi-skilled artisans and is conveniently 
located from Kolkata. A 5,000 square foot plot of 
land has been given to the Gem and Jewellery 
Trade Federation for the construction of a skill-
development centre within the park.
