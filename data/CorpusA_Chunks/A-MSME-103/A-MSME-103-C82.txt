The warehouses are to be appointed/ licensed at particular places only 
which have been so declared by Central Board of Excise and Customs. 
Warehouse can be defined as the commercial building or godown for 
storage of goods. It is used for storing the goods by traders that will be 
distributed later. It is mainly used for preventing losses and damages 
that may arise out of defective and unsecured storage. Traders usually 
store the cargo in a warehouse and get it released when it is required for 
manufacturing or sales.
