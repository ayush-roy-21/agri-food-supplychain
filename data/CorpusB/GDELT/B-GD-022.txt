FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
A consignment of premium Kesar mangoes exported by a Pune-based farmer producer company was rejected by UK plant quarantine authorities at London Heathrow airport due to a mismatch between the physical box barcode and the online HortiNet database. APEDA's HortiNet system requires real-time linking of farmer harvest lots with packhouse phytosanitary treatment certificates. However, poor rural connectivity in Maharashtra's orchard belts often causes delayed data uploading, leading to technical non-compliance at destination ports even when the produce is physically free of quarantine pests.
