FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Under the Foreign Trade Policy 2023, Five-Star Export Houses such as Allanasons Private Limited benefit from green-channel customs clearance, self-certification of origin, and exemption from compulsory bank guarantees at Indian ports. A comparative analysis of processed food exports from Mumbai and Nhava Sheva indicates that while Allanasons Private Limited achieves port clearance within 24 hours, non-status MSME food processors undergo 100 percent physical consignment examination and documentary checks by customs and FSSAI port health officers, extending transit times by up to five working days.
