FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Facing recurring server synchronization lags between APEDA trade portals and customs automated systems, listed exporters GRM Overseas Limited and Kohinoor Foods Limited have deployed automated enterprise resource planning (ERP) bridges to streamline ICEGATE shipping bill filing. Industry observations confirm that while established corporate entities like GRM Overseas Limited and Kohinoor Foods Limited maintain dedicated customs desk personnel to resolve electronic data interchange (EDI) error codes instantly, standalone MSME rice exporters suffer 3-to-5 day container holds at Kandla and Mundra ports due to manual filing errors and system timeouts.
