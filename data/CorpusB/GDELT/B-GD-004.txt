FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
The upcoming European Union Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) is threatening to lock out thousands of Indian smallholder coffee, cocoa, and spice growers from European markets. Under the statutory mandate, exporters must submit geolocation polygon coordinates (GPS geotagging) for all plots of land where commodities were grown, proving zero deforestation after December 31, 2020. Indian Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and MSME exporters in Karnataka and Kerala lack the digital infrastructure, GIS mapping tools, and financial capital required to audit fragmented smallholder estates. While APEDA and the Coffee Board have initiated pilot projects for blockchain-based traceability, exporters warn that compliance verification costs will render Indian produce uncompetitive against larger corporate plantations in Latin America and Vietnam.
