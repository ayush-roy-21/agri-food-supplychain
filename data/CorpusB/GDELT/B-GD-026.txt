FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Exporters of perishable floriculture, exotic vegetables, and fresh fruits operating out of New Delhi's Indira Gandhi International Airport report severe margin erosion due to acute air cargo capacity shortages and skyrocketing freight rates. Following geopolitical airspace restrictions and seasonal tourist demand peaks, airlines have reduced dedicated freighter allocations for agricultural cargo. A Delhi-based MSME vegetable exporter noted that freight charges to London and Frankfurt have surged by 40 percent over three weeks, while cargo offloading and terminal handling delays at perishable cargo centres have increased post-harvest spoilage.
