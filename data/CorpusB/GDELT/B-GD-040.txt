FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Seafood exporting MSMEs in Balasore and Paradip, Odisha, report significant competitive disadvantages due to the total absence of NABL and EIC-accredited analytical testing laboratories in the state's coastal districts. To obtain mandatory pre-shipment health certificates and antibiotic screening reports required by buyers in the European Union and Vietnam, processors must courier frozen muscle samples to laboratories in Kolkata or Visakhapatnam. This logistical dependency adds between 5 to 7 days to export cycle times and increases sample transit spoilage risks during peak summer months.
