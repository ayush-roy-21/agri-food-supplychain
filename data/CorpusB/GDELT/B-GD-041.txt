FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
A comprehensive control audit published by the European Commission's Directorate-General for Health and Food Safety (DG SANTE) evaluated India's residue monitoring plan for aquaculture products. While commending the statutory framework administered by MPEDA and EIC, the audit identified gaps in official control oversight regarding the over-the-counter sale of veterinary medicinal products and unapproved aquaculture chemicals in farming clusters. The report warned that unless traceability and prescription-only veterinary controls are strictly enforced at the retail hatchery level, Indian aquaculture exports remain at risk of heightened EU border surveillance.
