FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
The United States Food and Drug Administration (US FDA) has placed several Indian marine food processing units under Detention Without Physical Examination (DWPE) due to the detection of banned veterinary antibiotic residues, including nitrofuran and chloramphenicol, as well as Salmonella contamination in frozen shrimp shipments. The Marine Products Export Development Authority (MPEDA) has intensified farm-level surveillance and made Pre-Harvest Testing (PHT) mandatory for aquaculture farms in Andhra Pradesh and Gujarat. However, small-scale aquaculture farmers report significant hurdles in accessing accredited testing labs and maintaining digital farm traceability logs required by US importers under the Food Safety Modernization Act (FSMA) Foreign Supplier Verification Programs (FSVP).
