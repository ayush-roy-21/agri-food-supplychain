FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Small and medium agricultural exporters are facing severe working capital shortages due to delays in the issuance of electronic scrips under the Remission of Duties and Taxes on Exported Products (RoDTEP) scheme. Exporters report that customs portals frequently freeze scrip generation during departmental audits and verification of Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) realization records. Furthermore, the rebate rates for value-added processed food products and marine items are considered insufficient to offset embedded municipal taxes, electricity duties, and transport fuel levies. MSME industry bodies have petitioned the Ministry of Finance and DGFT for an automated, paperless claim settlement mechanism to prevent export liquidity bottlenecks.
