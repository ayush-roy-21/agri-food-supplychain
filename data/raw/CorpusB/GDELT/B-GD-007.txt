FULL SUBSTANTIVE ARTICLE TEXT:
Newly registered agricultural start-ups and Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) attempting to export fresh fruits and vegetables are encountering severe digital onboarding hurdles. Recent statutory directives requiring seamless electronic integration between the APEDA Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC) and the DGFT Importer-Exporter Code (IEC) database have resulted in widespread authentication mismatches. When exporters modify their corporate address or director details on the DGFT portal, the APEDA TraceNet and HortiNet systems frequently fail to synchronize in real-time, blocking the issuance of mandatory phytosanitary certificates and Certificate of Origin (CoO) applications at inspection agencies.
