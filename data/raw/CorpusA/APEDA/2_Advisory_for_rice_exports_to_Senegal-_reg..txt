APEDA Agricultural and Processed Food Products
Export Development Authority
(Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Govt. of India)
File No.: CRL-2020-21-000031
Subject: Advisory for rice exports to Senegal- reg.
This is in reference to APEDA Advisory No. CRL-2020-21-000031 dated 21.11.2025
regarding temporary suspension of issuance of Registration-Cum-Allocation Certificates
(RCAC) for export of non-Basmati rice to Senegal due to suspension of issuance of
import declarations for rice (DIPA) by the Government of Senegal for a period of one
month.
The Embassy of India, Dakar, Senegal, and trade stakeholders/exporters have informed
that the aforesaid month-long restriction on import of rice has now been lifted.
In the light of the above, APEDA will resume the issuance of RCAC’s for export of non-
Basmati rice to the Senegal with immediate effect. However, stakeholders are also
requested to watch over the latest developments in Senegal for any change in the
policy conditions related to rice. In case of any doubt, it is advised to consult
Eol(Dakar)/APEDA, as a note of abundant caution, before deciding on the
shipment. \\,
