OFFICE ORDER PART II No: 06/2024
Sub: Constitution of the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) to deal
with complaints of sexual harassment of women at workplaces—reg.

In supersession to Office Order Part II No. 05/2021 dated 21.06.2021
and as per provisions of sub-section (1) of section 4, Chapter II of the Sexual
Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal)
Act, 2013, the Internal Complaints Committee is hereby re-constituted to deal
with the complaints of sexual harassment of women employees in MPEDA with
the following members: -
|. Smt. Elsamma Ithack, Deputy Director (Chairperson
2.Shri. Sreejith P. T, Assistant Director
3. Smt. Aneesa T. A, Assistant Director Member |
4. Smt. Riji R.K,Senior Clerk |
5. Women Advocate from the High Court of KeralaJExpert Member

The above committee shall deal with the prevention and redressal of
complaints of sexual harassment of women employees in MPEDA and matters
connected therewith or incidental thereto as per the provisions of Sexual
Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal)
Act, 2013 and other relevant Rules in force. The Chairperson shall receive the
complaints of sexual harassment, if any, on behalf of the ICC and shall co-
ordinate the deliberations of the ICC on the complaints received. The
Chairperson of ICC shall monitor the She-Box, which is an online portal of the
Complaint Management System for lodging complaints related to sexual
harassment at the workplace.

--- PAGE 2 ---
The Committee shall make an Annual Report to the undersigned in
addition to the periodic report on the action taken as provided in the guidelines
of the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and
Redressal) Rules, 2013.

The committee shall hold office for such a period not exceeding three years
from the date of issue of this order.
