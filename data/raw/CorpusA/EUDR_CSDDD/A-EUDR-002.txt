OFFICIAL JOURNAL OF THE EUROPEAN UNION
DIRECTIVE (EU) 2024/1760 OF THE EUROPEAN PARLIAMENT AND OF THE COUNCIL
on Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence and amending Directive (EU) 2019/1937 (CSDDD)

1. SCOPE OF SUPPLY CHAIN SUSTAINABILITY GOVERNANCE:
The Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) establishes statutory liability for large EU and non-EU companies to identify, prevent, mitigate, and account for adverse human rights and environmental impacts across their entire global chain of activities, specifically including upstream agricultural and food supply chains.

2. UPSTREAM AGRI-FOOD SOURCING AUDITS:
European food manufacturers and retailers importing Indian agricultural commodities (spices, tea, coffee, processed foods) must conduct mandatory due diligence audits of third-country suppliers, assessing compliance with international labor conventions, child labor prohibitions, and agrochemical pollution limits.

3. CONTRACTUAL ASSURANCES & CAPABILITY BUILDING:
Buying corporations are required to obtain cascading contractual warranties from Indian exporters and farmer producer organizations (FPOs), backed by independent third-party verification audits and targeted financial investments to support sustainable agricultural transitions.
