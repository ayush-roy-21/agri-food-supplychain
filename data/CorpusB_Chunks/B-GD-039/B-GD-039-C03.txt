Exporters argue that improving rail connectivity and expanding port infrastructure in eastern India would significantly strengthen export competitiveness and reduce logistics costs. Premium rice varieties from West Bengal, including Gobindo Bhog and Tulai Panji, are also witnessing increasing demand in both domestic and international markets due to their unique aroma and cultural significance. Industry voices suggest that targeted branding and global promotional campaigns could elevate their recognition and export value. Meanwhile, the West Bengal Rice Mills Owners Association has raised concerns over the impact of extensive free rice distribution under welfare schemes, arguing that large-scale coverage under both state and central programs is weakening market demand. They estimate that around 1,500 rice mills operate in the state, but many are either struggling or shut due to low profitability. Stakeholders believe that a more balanced policy approach, combined with stronger export focus, could help the state capture a larger share of India’s Rs 90,000 crore rice export industry.
