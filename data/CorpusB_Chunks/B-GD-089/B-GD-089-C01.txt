The Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) has introduced important amendments to the Foreign Trade Policy (FTP) 2023 and the Handbook of Procedures (HBP) 2023. These changes, announced via gazette notifications, primarily focus on strengthening the framework for issuance, verification, and digitalization of the Certificate of Origin (CoO). A CoO is an official document that certifies the country where goods are manufactured. It plays a critical role in international trade by enabling importing countries to determine applicable tariffs and regulatory treatment.
