Officials said Lulu Retail will procure at least 1,000 MT of apricots from farmers in Ladakh this season and handle processing, packaging, and international marketing while maintaining Ladakh’s identity. Currently, farmers face challenges in sorting, cleaning, packaging, and marketing apricots using traditional methods, often leading to heavy losses. “With the MoU in place, farmers will hand over their orchards at harvest time to the exporter, who will take care of plucking, sorting, processing, packaging, transportation, and marketing in international markets,” the spokesperson said. The Lieutenant Governor said this would ensure minimal losses and fair pricing for farmers. “The unique apricots of Ladakh are an agricultural heritage of the region.
