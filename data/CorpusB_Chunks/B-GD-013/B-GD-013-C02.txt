Union grew by 37.9 per cent in value and 35.2 per cent in volume. Southeast Asian markets also registered significant gains, with growth of over 36 per cent in value. Exports to Japan increased by 6.55 per cent in value, whereas shipments to West Asia saw a marginal decline of 0.55 per cent amid regional instability toward the end of the financial year. Among product categories, frozen fish, squid, cuttlefish, dried items, and live products recorded positive growth, while chilled products witnessed a decline. Surimi, fishmeal, and fish oil exports also improved. In terms of logistics, five major ports--Visakhapatnam, JNPT, Kochi, Kolkata, and Chennai--accounted for nearly 64 per cent of total export value, underlining their importance in India's seafood export ecosystem.
