SPICES BOARD CIRCULAR NO: 05/2025-26
SUBJECT: Requirement of Official Certificate for Export of Spices & Spice Products to United Kingdom (UK) w.e.f. 1st January 2026

1. REGULATORY BACKGROUND:
Pursuant to UK Official Controls (England) Regulations and Food Standards Agency (FSA) enhanced surveillance requirements for high-risk food and feed imported from third countries, new border compliance protocols take effect starting 1st January 2026.

2. OFFICIAL CERTIFICATE MANDATE:
All export consignments of Indian spices and spice products shipped to the United Kingdom on or after 1st January 2026 must be accompanied by an Official Health Certificate issued by competent statutory authorities (Spices Board / Export Inspection Council).

3. MANDATORY TESTING PARAMETERS:
Prior to endorsement of the Official Certificate, consignments must undergo rigorous pre-shipment analytical verification at Spices Board Quality Evaluation Laboratories (QEL) covering:
- Ethylene Oxide (ETO) and 2-Chloroethanol residues (Limit of Quantification compliance)
- Pesticide Residues (UK MRL compliance across multi-residue screens)
- Mycotoxins (Aflatoxin B1 and Total Aflatoxins) and Salmonella spp.

4. IMPLEMENTATION & COMPLIANCE:
Exporters are instructed to submit online applications via the Exporter Service System (ESS) / ICEGATE integrated portal at least 5 working days prior to container stuffing. Consignments lacking the Official Certificate will face mandatory rejection at UK Border Control Posts (BCPs).
