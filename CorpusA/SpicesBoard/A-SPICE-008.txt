SPICES BOARD CIRCULAR
SUBJECT: Export of Cumin to European Union (EU) - Monitoring Mechanism for Testing of Pesticide Residues (PR)

1. RATIONALE:
Following Rapid Alert System for Food and Feed (RASFF) notifications regarding potential risks associated with pesticide residue non-compliances in Cumin seeds (Cuminum cyminum) exported to European Union member states, Spices Board has instituted a specialized monitoring and testing mechanism.

2. MONITORING REGIME FOR CUMIN EXPORTS:
All registered exporters shipping Cumin (whole, ground, or processed) to the European Union must subject every export lot to mandatory multi-pesticide residue analytical testing prior to customs clearance.

3. ANALYTICAL PARAMETERS & EU MRL STRICTNESS:
Testing must verify strict compliance with EU Default MRLs (Regulation EC No 396/2005 as amended), with specific focus on:
- Chlorpyrifos, Carbendazim, Profenofos, Thiamethoxam, Triazophos, and Acetamiprid
- Ethylene Oxide (ETO) and associated reaction products.

4. CLEARANCE PROCEDURE:
Only lots achieving a 'Cleared' status from Spices Board QEL will be issued the pre-shipment clearance certificate. Exporters are advised to implement backward traceability and raw material testing at farm-gate level to minimize lot failures.
