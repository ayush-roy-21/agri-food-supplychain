THE MARINE PRODUCTS EXPORT DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (MPEDA)
STATUTORY COMPLIANCE PROCEDURE: NOAA FORM DS-2031 EXPORTER DECLARATION FOR USA SHRIMP EXPORTS

1. BACKGROUND & US REGULATORY MANDATE:
Pursuant to Section 609 of United States Public Law 101-162, all shipments of shrimp and shrimp products imported into the United States must be accompanied by the Exporter's / Importer's Declaration (Form DS-2031) issued by the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA).

2. CERTIFICATION OF AQUACULTURE VS. WILD-CAUGHT SHRIMP:
Since India's wild-caught shrimp trawl fishery faces Section 609 TED restrictions, US customs clearances rely on two distinct certification pathways:
- Aquaculture / Farmed Shrimp (Box 7(A)(2)): Shrimp harvested from aquaculture facilities where sea turtles do not occur. Exporters must attach MPEDA enrollment farm registration certificates and Pre-Harvest Test (PHT) lot codes proving farmed origin.
- Wild-Caught Shrimp harvested using Turtle Excluder Devices (TEDs): Requires explicit endorsement by government-authorized inspection officers certifying that trawlers operated approved TED gear.

3. MANDATORY DOCUMENTATION PACKET:
For every US container shipment, processing plants must submit to MPEDA/EIC:
- Complete DS-2031 Form signed by responsible corporate officer
- Farm Traceability Sheet linking pond enrollment numbers to processing lot codes
- Bill of Lading and Commercial Invoice matching container sealing records.
