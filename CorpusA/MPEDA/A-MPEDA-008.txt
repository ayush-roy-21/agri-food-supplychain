THE MARINE PRODUCTS EXPORT DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (MPEDA)
PRE-HARVEST TEST (PHT) SYSTEM MANUAL FOR ANTIBIOTIC RESIDUE MONITORING IN AQUACULTURE

1. OBJECTIVE:
To eliminate zero-tolerance banned antibiotic residues (specifically Chloramphenicol and Nitrofuran metabolites AOZ, AMOZ, AHD, SEM) in farmed shrimp (Litopenaeus vannamei and Penaeus monodon) exported to the European Union, United States, and Japan.

2. PRE-HARVEST SAMPLING PROTOCOL:
Mandatory sampling must occur prior to harvest at enrolled aquaculture farms.
- Sampling is executed by MPEDA / NaCSA authorized sample collectors or ELISA screening laboratories.
- Samples must be drawn strictly within 10 to 15 days prior to the scheduled harvest date.
- A composite sample of 500 grams must be sealed in tamper-evident sample bags with unique barcoded PHT tags.

3. ANALYTICAL TESTING STANDARDS:
Screening is performed at MPEDA Quality Control Laboratories (QCL) using Liquid Chromatography-Tandem Mass Spectrometry (LC-MS/MS) achieving Minimum Required Performance Limits (MRPL) of 0.3 ppb for Chloramphenicol and 1.0 ppb for Nitrofurans.

4. ONLINE PHT CERTIFICATE ISSUANCE & TRACEABILITY:
Upon achieving 'Negative / Undetected' analytical clearance, the online PHT certificate is automatically generated in the e-MPEDA portal. Processing plants are strictly prohibited from receiving farm shrimp lots lacking a valid barcoded PHT clearance certificate.
