THE MARINE PRODUCTS EXPORT DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (MPEDA)
(Ministry of Commerce & Industry, Government of India)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP) FOR ISSUANCE & VALIDATION OF EU CATCH CERTIFICATE

1. LEGAL & REGULATORY MANDATE:
Under Council Regulation (EC) No 1005/2008 establishing a community system to prevent, deter and eliminate Illegal, Unreported and Unregulated (IUU) fishing, marine fishery products imported into the European Union must be accompanied by a validated Catch Certificate. MPEDA is designated by the Government of India as the competent validating authority.

2. TRACEABILITY & FISHING VESSEL VERIFICATION:
Exporters applying for an EU Catch Certificate must upload verifiable landing records linking the raw material to authorized Indian fishing vessels registered with the Real Craft portal / Department of Fisheries.
Key verification parameters include:
- Fishing Vessel Registration Number and Gear Type
- Date and Geographical Area of Catch (FAO Fishing Area 51/57)
- Landing Port and Verification of Logbooks.

3. DIGITAL VALIDATION WORKFLOW:
All applications must be processed online through the MPEDA Catch Certificate portal (e-mpeda.nic.in). Designated MPEDA officers conduct physical and documentation verification at processing plants prior to digital stamping and signature endorsement.

4. RECORD RETENTION & AUDIT:
Exporters and MPEDA regional offices must preserve complete traceability dossiers (landing receipts, transport bills, lot codes) for a minimum period of five years for bilateral European Commission food safety audits.
