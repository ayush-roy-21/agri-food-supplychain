MINISTRY OF COMMERCE & INDUSTRY / AGRICULTURAL AND PROCESSED FOOD PRODUCTS EXPORT DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (APEDA)
EXECUTIVE ADVISORY: IMPLEMENTATION OF DIGITAL GEOLOCATION TRACEABILITY FOR EU DEFORESTATION REGULATION (EUDR) COMPLIANCE

1. CRITICAL IMPACT ON INDIAN AGRI-FOOD EXPORTS:
Recognizing the impending enforcement of Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 (EUDR), the Department of Commerce mandates immediate digital preparedness for Indian exporters handling coffee, cocoa, palm oil derivatives, and paper/wooden food packaging shipped to European destinations.

2. INTEGRATION OF APEDA TRACENET WITH GPS PLOT MAPPING:
To prevent trade disruptions at European ports, APEDA is upgrading its TraceNet digital infrastructure to capture farm-level geolocation polygons. Registered exporters must upload GPS coordinates verified by State Horticulture Departments or remote sensing satellite imagery for all registered contract farming plots.

3. INTER-OPERABILITY WITH EU TRACES PORTAL:
The advisory establishes guidelines for Indian exporters to generate standardized GeoJSON plot dossiers capable of automated transmission into the European Commission's TRACES information system when generating pre-shipment export documentation.
